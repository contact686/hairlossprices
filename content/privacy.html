This site collects as little as it can.

It is a static site with no accounts, no forms and no advertising pixels. Hosting logs may record the IP address and browser of visitors for a short time. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives a request from your browser when a page loads. If analytics are added they will be listed here first. Links to providers may include a tracking code so the provider can attribute a signup; what the provider collects after you leave this site is governed by its own privacy policy.
